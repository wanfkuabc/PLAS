
\begin{abstract}
	Referring image segmentation (RIS) requires grounding
	language expressions in images and recovering pixel-accurate
	masks. Existing multimodal large language model (MLLM)
	segmenters often bottleneck dense mask geometry at the
	interface between multimodal reasoning and mask decoding,
	limiting spatial detail and efficiency. We propose
	\textbf{PLAS}, an efficient RIS framework based on learnable
	multimodal feature readout and progressive mask-latent
	alignment. PLAS introduces learnable spatial readout tokens
	into the MLLM and extracts multimodal features in parallel
	from multiple transformer layers. A progressive teacher--student
	framework transfers mask geometry through teacher pretraining,
	latent warmup, and image-conditioned adaptation. The readout
	features are fused with visual-grid information, aligned with
	teacher mask latents, and converted into dense prompts for SAM
	decoding. Experiments on RefCOCO, RefCOCO+, RefCOCOg, and
	gRefCOCO show that PLAS matches or surpasses leading
	segmentation methods while achieving a model-side latency of
	\textbf{0.43~s per image}. It is \textbf{1.63$\times$} faster than
	the fastest compared method and \textbf{2.49$\times$} faster
	than a method with comparable segmentation accuracy.
\end{abstract}
